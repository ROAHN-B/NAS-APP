Find, set up and open your Media Server

Open this page inside the NAS Controller app. Finding devices on Wi-Fi needs the app.

My devices

Add a device

Connect this phone to the same Wi-Fi as the NAS (or turn on this phone's hotspot if the NAS uses it), then scan.

  1. Power on the NAS. If it has no saved Wi-Fi, it shows Configure WiFi on its screen.
  2. Open this phone's Wi-Fi settings and join ESP32-NAS-Setup (password 12345678). Android may open the setup page by itself.
  3. If it doesn't, come back here and tap Open setup page.
  4. Pick your home Wi-Fi, enter its password and save. The NAS restarts and joins it.
  5. Reconnect this phone to your home Wi-Fi and use Find on Wi-Fi.

Use the IP on the NAS screen at home, or your Cloudflare address (e.g. media-server.app) to reach it from anywhere.
